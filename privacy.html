Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Support Privacy

Summary: Mapped Camera Pins does not collect personal data on developer servers. Apple’s nutrition label for this app is intended as Data Not Collected. Location and camera use stay on your device for the features you turn on.

Who we are

Mapped Camera Pins is an independent civic transparency iOS app by Stephen Moore. It is not affiliated with Flock Safety or any ALPR vendor.

Contact: stephenmoorecm1357@gmail.com

Data we do not collect

We do not operate developer analytics, accounts, or backend servers that receive your personal information. We do not sell data.

Location

If you grant location permission, the app uses it on-device to show nearby mapped pins, Drive Mode / Live Activity, route comparison, and optional pin-zone alerts. Location is not sent to the developer.

Camera

If you use AR Camera Sight, the camera overlays mapped OSM ALPR locations. Video is not recorded or uploaded by the app.

Map and related network requests

  • OpenStreetMap / Overpass: map pin queries for community-tagged ALPR nodes (no user account IDs from us).
  • Optional traffic stills: only if you open a Sensor Atlas detail that loads a public traveler still from allowlisted WisDOT hosts — not ALPR/Flock live feeds; not recorded by the app.

Purchases

Optional tip jar purchases go through Apple In-App Purchase. Apple processes payment; we do not receive your full payment card details.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
