What this app is

Flock Surveillance shows community-documented automatic license plate reader (ALPR) locations from OpenStreetMap, plus related on-device tools (Drive Mode, route pin scores, Place Score, Sharing Network samples, AR overlay of mapped pins).

Alerts and Live Activity mean your phone is near a mapped OSM pin — not that a plate was read.

Common questions

  • Is this a Flock Safety product? No. Not affiliated with Flock Safety. “Flock” may appear only as an OSM vendor/filter tag on mapped data.
  • Does it read plates or show live camera feeds? No.
  • Where does location go? On-device for map, Drive Mode, and optional pin-zone alerts. Location is not sent to developer servers.
  • Coverage looks incomplete. That’s expected — the map is what volunteers tagged, not a vendor inventory.
